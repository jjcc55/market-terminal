About This Dashboard

Market Terminal is a live screening dashboard that monitors ~500 S&P 500 + MidCap 400 stocks, ~94 ETFs, and 11 macro commodity proxies. Data is pulled automatically via a Google Apps Script that refreshes prices and recalculates signals. The dashboard reads live from the sheet every time you open or refresh it — no manual export needed.

Stock Signals

GUARDED DIP
Dip within a healthy range — the stock has dropped between 5% and 25% from its 30-day peak, while still meeting quality filters (Market Cap ≥ $8B, P/E ≤ 55). Targets quality names that have pulled back but haven't broken down.
MOMENTUM
Trend confirmation — price is within 5% of its 30-day high AND more than 8% above its 100-day moving average, with the same quality filters (MCap ≥ $8B, P/E ≤ 55). Flags names showing sustained momentum worth following.
HOLD
No active signal — the stock doesn't meet the thresholds for either a dip buy or momentum alert. Could be range-bound, in a deeper drawdown, or outside the quality filters.

ETF Signals

BUY FUND DIP
Dip above the trend line — the ETF has dropped 3–8% from its 30-day high while still trading above its 100-day moving average. A tactical entry into a fund that remains in an uptrend structurally.
ETF TREND UP
Trend strength confirmed — price is within 2% of its 30-day high AND more than 5% above its 100-day MA. The fund is showing broad-based momentum with minimal pullback.
HOLD
Neutral or below the trend — ETF is either in a neutral zone or trading below its 100-day MA, which disqualifies it from both signals regardless of other conditions.

Commodity Signals

BUY CYCLICAL DIP
Macro accumulation zone — the commodity proxy has dropped 6–18% from its 30-day peak AND is within 15% of its 52-week low. Targets cyclical assets at historically attractive re-entry points.
STRUCTURAL UPTREND
Macro tailwind intact — price is above the 30-day, 100-day, and 200-day moving averages, confirming a sustained structural uptrend across multiple timeframes.
